OpenRouter API key

By default your key stays in this browser tab’s session storage. Remembering it stores a persistent credential in this browser, accessible to scripts and anyone using this browser profile. Do not remember it on a shared device. It is sent through this app to OpenRouter when you chat. It is not saved to your Dayfold account. OpenRouter usage is billed to the account for the key you use. Avoid sharing it in chat.

Good to know

Can I share or link accounts?

No. Dayfold is for individual accounts. Each sign-in provider identity is separate; accounts cannot be linked or shared.

How do reminders reach me?

Reminders appear in your Dayfold chat, not as push notifications or email. Coming up shows the next scheduled occurrence.

What does the AI see?

Chat uses OpenRouter and its model provider after you opt in. The full AI data notice is shown before chatting. Saved full task descriptions are not sent to the model. Do not put secrets in chat.

Can I export data or delete my account?

There is no in-app export or account deletion. Removing your sign-in identity does not delete your Dayfold data.
